# My Favorite Movie: Now You See Me

**Now You See Me** is a 2013 heist thriller film directed by Louis Leterrier. It follows an FBI agent and an Interpol detective who track a team of illusionists known as **The Four Horsemen** as they pull off daring bank heists during their live performances and shower the stolen money onto their audiences.

---

## Why I Love It
* **Clever Illusions & Twists:** The magic tricks feel grand and theatrical, and the plot keeps you guessing until the final reveal of the "Fifth Horseman."
* **Charismatic Cast:** The chemistry among the four magicians makes every stage show and getaway entertaining to watch.
* **Fast Pacing:** The cat-and-mouse chase between the magicians and the authorities never slows down.

* **Release Year:** 2013
